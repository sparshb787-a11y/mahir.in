FAQ

Questions,
answered plainly.

The essentials about who we are, how we work, and what to expect before you speak with us.

What does the firm actually do?
We are a SEBI-registered investment advisory and market intelligence firm. We help long-term investors build portfolios grounded in original research, disciplined risk, and clear reasoning, not noise or short-term recommendations.
Are you registered with SEBI?
Yes. The firm operates a Separately Identifiable Investment Advisory Unit registered with SEBI (RIA Reg. No. INA000022668) and enlisted with BSE (No. 2526). Registration is valid from June 01, 2026, Perpetual.
Who is this for?
Individuals, families, and institutions who take investing seriously and want a research-led partner focused on process, transparency, and a long horizon rather than product-pushing.
How do you charge?
Fees are advisory-only and disclosed transparently before onboarding, in line with SEBI's IA regulations. We do not earn commissions or brokerage on client transactions.
What is the screening tool?
The screener is a market intelligence product — a research toolkit for filtering, comparing, and understanding listed companies. It complements the advisory practice and is available at mahirscreener.com.
